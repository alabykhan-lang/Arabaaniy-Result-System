Access rules03
Administrator section

Admin: prepare the system

1

Register staff

Create or confirm staff accounts and keep access personal to each user.

2

Set the academic context

Confirm session, term, classes, subjects, grading and report-card settings.

3

Confirm students

Check class lists, names, photos and active student records.

4

Assign work

Make sure teachers know the classes and subjects they are handling.

Administrator manual04
Teacher section

Teacher: complete academic work

  • Sign in with your own account.
  • Open the assigned class and confirm the students.
  • Enter continuous assessment and examination scores.
  • Complete permitted traits and remarks.
  • Review entries, save and report completion.

Teacher manual05
Administrator section

Admin: review and approve

  • Check that all expected students and subjects are complete.
  • Review scores, totals, grades, traits and remarks.
  • Resolve missing or incorrect entries.
  • Approve or publish the completed result.

Only after review should the result move to report-card production.

Report-card workflow07
Training plan

Three layers of orientation

1. Manuals

Short admin and teacher references for daily use.

2. Slides and videos

One visual lesson per major task, about 3–7 minutes each.

3. Checklists

Simple prompts used during live training and the first result cycle.

Recommended order

Registration → setup → teacher entry → review → approval → report card.

Handover orientation08
Completion check

Before the result cycle closes

  • All students are in the correct classes.
  • All assigned entries are complete.
  • Errors have been resolved.
  • Results have been reviewed and approved.
  • Report cards have been checked and printed by an administrator.

When these five checks are complete, the school is ready for handover of the final output.
