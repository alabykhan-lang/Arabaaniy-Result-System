# School Result Portal — Teacher Manual

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the School Result Portal.
2. Sign in with your own teacher account.
3. Confirm that your assigned work area and classes are available.

Do not use another teacher's account or share your password.

## 2. Confirm the class and students

1. Select the class you are working on.
2. Check the student list.
3. Report missing students or wrong class assignments to the administrator.
4. Do not change school-wide settings.

## 3. Enter academic records

For each assigned student and subject, enter the permitted continuous assessment and examination scores. Complete traits, remarks or other academic fields assigned to you.

Check every number before saving. Keep the original school record available while entering data.

## 4. Review your entries

Before submission, check:

- student names;
- subject and class;
- CA and examination scores;
- totals and grades;
- traits and remarks;
- blank or obviously incorrect values.

Correct your entries while correction is available. Report locked or published errors to the administrator.

## 5. Submit completed work

Tell the administrator when your class or subject is complete. Do not assume that saving one score means the entire result is ready for approval.

## 6. Report-card boundary

Teachers do not open Settings and do not print report cards. The administrator reviews the completed result and handles report-card printing.

If you need a correction or need to confirm a result, contact the administrator.

## 7. Teacher completion checklist

- Correct class selected.
- All expected students checked.
- All assigned scores entered.
- Traits and remarks completed where required.
- Entries reviewed for mistakes.
- Completion reported to the administrator.
