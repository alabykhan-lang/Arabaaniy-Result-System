# Orientation Checklists

## Administrator live-training checklist

- [ ] Sign in successfully.
- [ ] Confirm management and Settings are visible.
- [ ] Register or confirm a teacher account.
- [ ] Confirm session, term, classes and subjects.
- [ ] Confirm a sample student record.
- [ ] Review a sample teacher entry.
- [ ] Approve or publish the sample result.
- [ ] Open Result Cards.
- [ ] Preview a card.
- [ ] Print one card and confirm the output.

## Teacher live-training checklist

- [ ] Sign in successfully.
- [ ] Confirm assigned class/work area.
- [ ] Confirm the sample student list.
- [ ] Enter sample CA and examination scores.
- [ ] Review totals and grades.
- [ ] Save and report completion.
- [ ] Confirm Settings is not available.
- [ ] Confirm report-card print controls are not available.

## Result-cycle checklist

- [ ] Registration/class lists confirmed.
- [ ] Teacher assignments confirmed.
- [ ] Academic entries completed.
- [ ] Missing or incorrect entries corrected.
- [ ] Administrator review completed.
- [ ] Results approved/published.
- [ ] Report cards previewed.
- [ ] Report cards printed by administrator.
