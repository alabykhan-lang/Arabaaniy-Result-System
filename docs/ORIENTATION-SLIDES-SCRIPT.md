# Orientation Slides and Video Script

Use one short section per slide or video. Demonstrate with approved sample data first.

## Slide 1 — Welcome

“This portal takes the school from student registration to the final report card. We will follow the same order used during each result cycle.”

## Slide 2 — The complete workflow

Registration → session and class setup → teacher assignment → academic entry → review → approval → report-card printing.

## Slide 3 — Two access levels

“Teachers do the normal academic entry and review work. Administrators control Settings, review/approval and report-card printing.”

## Slide 4 — Registration

Show the sign-in and registration/invitation process. Explain that every staff member uses a personal account.

## Slide 5 — Academic setup (administrator)

Show session, term, classes, subjects, grading and report-card settings. Explain that teachers do not see this area.

## Slide 6 — Student and class records

Show how to confirm the class list and report missing or incorrect students.

## Slide 7 — Teacher score entry

Show selecting a class, entering CA/exam scores, saving, reviewing and reporting completion.

## Slide 8 — Review and approval

Show the administrator checking completeness, totals, grades, traits and remarks before approval/publication.

## Slide 9 — Report card

Show the administrator selecting a student or class, refreshing data, checking the preview and printing. State clearly that teacher accounts cannot print.

## Slide 10 — Closing checklist

“Before the result cycle closes: all students are correct, all assigned entries are complete, errors are resolved, results are approved and report cards are checked before printing.”

## Suggested recordings

- Video 1: Registration and sign-in — 3 minutes
- Video 2: Administrator setup — 5 minutes
- Video 3: Teacher entry — 5 minutes
- Video 4: Administrator review and approval — 5 minutes
- Video 5: Report-card printing — 3 minutes
