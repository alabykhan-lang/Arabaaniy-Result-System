# School Result Portal — Administrator Manual

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the School Result Portal.
2. Sign in with the administrator account.
3. Confirm that the management and Settings areas are visible.

If Settings or management controls are missing, do not change records. Sign out and contact the system administrator.

## 2. Register and manage staff

1. Open the staff registration or invitation process.
2. Give each approved teacher the current invitation code or registration instruction.
3. Ask the teacher to complete registration with their own account.
4. Open Management Access to confirm the new account.
5. Assign the correct staff role and, where configured, the appropriate class/subject scope.
6. Suspend or restore access when necessary.

Never share one account between teachers.

## 3. Prepare the academic session

In Settings, confirm the school information, academic session, term, classes, subjects, grading rules, assessment columns, remarks and report-card template. Save changes before teachers begin entry.

The academic context should be checked at the start of every term.

## 4. Confirm student registration

1. Open the student/class area.
2. Add or review each student.
3. Confirm the student name, class, photo and other required details.
4. Archive only records that should no longer appear in active class work.
5. Confirm that every expected student appears in the correct class.

## 5. Monitor teacher entry

Teachers enter the academic records for their assigned work. The administrator should monitor completion, check unusual or missing scores, and ask teachers to correct errors before final review.

## 6. Review and publish results

1. Open the relevant class and term.
2. Review scores, totals, grades, traits and remarks.
3. Confirm that required subjects are complete.
4. Approve or publish the completed result according to the school process.
5. If an error is found after submission, return it for correction or make the authorized correction.

## 7. Generate and print report cards

1. Open Result Cards.
2. Select the class, term, session and student, or choose the class list.
3. Refresh the data.
4. Check the displayed report card before printing.
5. Choose Print Card or Print All.
6. Select the correct printer or PDF destination and confirm the output.

Teachers may view result information allowed by their access, but report-card printing is an administrator function.

## 8. End-of-term checklist

- All students are in the correct classes.
- Required subjects have complete entries.
- Teachers have submitted their work.
- Results have been reviewed and approved.
- Report cards have been checked and printed.
- The next term is not activated until the current term is complete.
- Staff access and invitation settings remain correct.
